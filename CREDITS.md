# Credits

AO-Greenroom exists because of the work of many people. Thank you. 💚

## Core contributors & ideas

- **OmniTroid** — the original 3D (MMD) character support in
  [LemmyAO PR #53](https://github.com/SyntaxNyah/LemmyAO/pull/53): the
  `babylon-mmd` integration, mouth-vowel morphs, skeleton-based auto-framing,
  and the "Green Room" workbench concept. Also author of the
  [aolib-meta](https://github.com/OmniTroid/aolib-meta) asset schemas
  (`CameraRig`, `CharIni`) that define the `camera.json` and `char.ini` formats
  this tool writes.
- **ChocomintCake (SyntaxNyah)** — author of
  [Pinsel-AO-Char-Maker](https://github.com/SyntaxNyah/Pinsel-AO-Char-Maker),
  owner of [LemmyAO](https://github.com/SyntaxNyah/LemmyAO), and initiator of
  this project.

## Libraries

- **[babylon.js](https://www.babylonjs.com/)** (Babylon team) — the WebGL
  engine (`@babylonjs/core`).
- **[babylon-mmd](https://github.com/noname0310/babylon-mmd)** (noname0310) —
  the MMD (PMX/VMD) loader and runtime that makes this possible.
- **Vite** and **Vitest** — build tooling and test runner.
- **Tauri** — the desktop shell.

## Community & format references

- The **Attorney Online** development team and community — the AO2 format, the
  webAO client, and the conventions this tool targets.
- **KFO-CharMaker** and **DROButtonMaker** — inspiration for the "idiot-proof
  character maker" philosophy carried over from Pinsel.

If you've contributed and aren't listed, open a PR and add yourself.
